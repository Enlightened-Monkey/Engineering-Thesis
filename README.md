# Engineering-Thesis

## Research Paper Summary

This repository contains research materials related to reinforcement learning and behavioral economics.

### Available Documents

- **[1285_MS_Summary.md](./1285_MS_Summary.md)** - Comprehensive summary (in English and Polish) of the research paper "Teaching Precommitted Agents: Model-Free Policy Evaluation and Control in Quasi-Hyperbolic Discounted MDPs" by S.R. Eshwar

- **1285_MS (1).pdf** - Original research paper on quasi-hyperbolic discounting in reinforcement learning

## Research Focus

The main research focuses on:
- Time-inconsistent preferences in reinforcement learning
- Quasi-Hyperbolic (QH) discounting models
- Model-free algorithms for precommitted agents
- Applications in inventory control and behavioral decision-making
